How it works

Three steps from need to donation

1. Create your account

Sign up as a donor or a receiver in under a minute. Pin your location on the map so matches are truly nearby.

2. Search or send an alert

Receivers search available donors by blood group, city and distance, or broadcast an emergency request to everyone compatible.

3. Connect and donate

Donors accept or decline in one tap. When someone accepts, their contact details appear instantly on the request.

Built for everyone involved

One platform, three dashboards

Donors

  • Switch availability on or off
  • See compatible requests near you
  • Accept or decline in one tap
  • Donation history and next-eligible date

Receivers

  • Search donors by group, city and radius
  • Send emergency or direct requests
  • Track every response in real time
  • Find nearby blood banks and camps

Admins

  • Manage donors, users and requests
  • Maintain blood banks and stock levels
  • Publish donation camps
  • Full platform activity feed
